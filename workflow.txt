1. Developer works on Dev branch, commits and merge into Dev branch every day.
2. once the Dev branch is ready for the day, nightly regressions run.
3. nightly regressions have few visual tests, that publishes results in Eyes.
4. Every day QA team, reviews the results and raises bug on Jira.
5. there is a weekly release, every week, just before the release, regression runs again.
6. Publishes results to Eyes platform for QA team to review.
7. After the review, it gets released to Prod and the bugs are logged for the version to get fixed in next version.
8. Jira ticket contains details about the tasks assigned to developers.
9. There exist a validation guidlines on Confluence platform.
